# Seamless Connect industry events calendar

This is a curated, informational calendar of events relevant to Seamless Connect's work across domain registration, DNS, Internet standards, cloud and hosting platforms, and network infrastructure. Inclusion does **not** indicate that Seamless Connect, its contributors, or its sponsors plan to attend, speak, sponsor, or exhibit.

Dates and locations were checked against organizer sources on **22 September 2026**. Organizer links remain the source of truth; confirm details before making plans. Events with only a month or year listed have not announced exact dates.

## Announcements to watch

These recurring or expected events are relevant, but their next dates or complete logistics were not published when this page was checked:

- [ICANN Contracted Parties Summit](https://www.icann.org/en/contracted-parties/summit): a concentrated operational and business forum for ICANN-accredited registrars, gTLD registries, and ICANN staff. The published page currently covers the April 2026 summit; no 2027 edition is listed yet.
- [Registration Operations Workshop (ROW)](https://regiops.net/): online, highly technical registry/registrar operations sessions. ROW15 is listed above; no later session is announced yet.
- [CENTR events and Jamboree](https://www.centr.org/events.html): European ccTLD registry meetings and cross-functional workshops. The 2027 schedule is not yet public; some meetings are limited to members or invited participants.
- [DNS-OARC workshops](https://indico.dns-oarc.net/): semiannual DNS operations and research workshops. No 2027 workshop dates are published yet.
- [Verisign Registrar Days](https://www.verisign.com/en_US/channel-resources/domain-registry-products/registrar-resources/index.xhtml): registrar-focused relationship and operations events; future dates are typically announced directly to the registrar community.
- [Domain Days Dubai](https://www.domaindays.com/): the organizer postponed the announced October 2026 edition and has not yet published replacement dates.
- [TLDCON](https://tldcon.ru/en/): annual Eastern Europe and Central Asia ccTLD registry/registrar conference. The 2027 edition is not yet announced.
- [NamesCon Global](https://namescon.com/): the November 2026 edition is listed above; no 2027 dates are published yet.

Keep this repository page canonical. Update it when an organizer announces or changes a date, location, access restriction, or event status, and then mirror the material change on the public website.
