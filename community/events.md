# Seamless Connect industry events calendar

This calendar covers **22 September 2026 through 22 September 2027**. It prioritizes events where Seamless Connect can advance open standards, recruit implementers and pilot partners, and build relationships with DNS, domain, cloud, and Internet infrastructure operators.

Priorities are planning guidance for Seamless Connect, not endorsements of an event or organizer. Dates and locations were last checked against organizer sources on **22 September 2026**; confirm them with the organizer before booking travel.

## Planning guidance

- **Protect the standards track:** budget for IETF 127, 128, and 129 when the relevant work is active; prepare contributions and bilateral meetings in advance.
- **Choose conflicts deliberately:** NANOG 98 is the stronger implementation and operator opportunity; ICANN87 is stronger for registrar, registry, and policy relationships. RIPE 93 is the stronger fit for core Internet operators; Internet2 TechEX26 is more useful for research-and-education and shared-infrastructure partners.
- **Use CloudFest Americas + NamesCon as the main commercial ecosystem event:** arrive with a short pilot offer, target-account list, and scheduled meetings.
- **Stack travel where practical:** APRICOT 2027 leads into IETF 128, and any organizer-confirmed DNS-OARC workshop adjacent to RIPE 94 could create another efficient technical trip.
- **Keep attendance outcome-driven:** for each event, name an owner, target organizations, desired meetings, and the artifact to bring back (partner lead, implementation commitment, standards contribution, or deployment feedback).

## Maintenance

Open a pull request when an organizer changes a date or location, or when Seamless Connect commits to attend, speak, sponsor, or host a side meeting. Keep organizer links as the source of truth for logistics.
